COMPANY LEAVE AND PROBATION POLICY
Effective date: 1 January 2026

Annual Leave
Employees receive 18 annual leave days per calendar year. Leave is tracked
from January through December.

Leave Carry-Forward
A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried forward into the next
calendar year. Any additional unused annual leave expires at year end.

Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 sick leave days per calendar year. Employees should tell
their manager as soon as practical when they cannot work because of illness.

Requesting Routine Leave
Employees should submit routine leave requests at least 3 working days in
advance. A manager confirms approval.

Probation
The probation period lasts 3 months from the employee's start date.

Other Leave
This policy does not specify maternity leave entitlements. Employees should
consult the applicable company benefits documents for other leave categories.
